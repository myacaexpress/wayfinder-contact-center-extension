Wayfinder Call Protection

Automatic protection between AllCalls and Wayfinder. No per-call mode switches.

On pauses the other system during calls. Off lets you manage availability yourself.

Checking protection…

Keep your AllCalls Take Calls page and one signed-in Wayfinder agent workspace open in this Chrome profile.

Ringing, calls and wrap-up pause the other system. Only availability changed by protection is restored. Your own Break/Offline choices remain yours. Keep the calling pages open and the Mac awake.

AllCalls closed or logged out: Wayfinder works normally. An open AllCalls page with unreadable status needs attention. UI synchronization cannot retract a call already offered by either provider.
